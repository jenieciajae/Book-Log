About Me!

Jeniecia’s Book Log is a personal reading journal where I keep track of the books I’ve read and the ones I want to explore.

I created this site as a college student learning front-end development and UI/UX design. Instead of using a regular app, I wanted to build something of my own that reflects my style while keeping my reading experience organized.

Through my book logs, reviews, and recommendations, I hope to inspire other readers to explore new books and find stories they connect with.

This project is a mix of creativity and growth, allowing me to document my reading journey while continuing to develop my skills as a designer and developer.
