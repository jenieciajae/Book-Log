About the Creator

Jeniecia’s Book Log is a digital reading companion designed to help readers organize their libraries, discover new books, track their progress, and reflect on their reading experiences.

I created this project as a college student studying Digital Media and UX/UI Design while learning front-end development. My goal was to combine thoughtful design with functional features to create a reading experience that feels organized, simple, and enjoyable.

Through features like book organization, search, reading insights, and reviews, this project explores how technology can support the way people discover and interact with stories.

This project represents my growth as a designer and developer, combining my interest in books, user experience, and building meaningful digital experiences.
